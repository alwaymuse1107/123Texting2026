Find answers about Voice API, SMS/MMS, phone numbers, delivery status and integration. So your team can ship faster.

What can I build with your Voice API?

With our Voice API, you can programmatically make and receive phone calls, create IVR menus, route calls to teams, record calls, and power real-time voice workflows. Common use cases include customer support lines, call centers, OTP verification calls, and outbound sales calls.

Do you support inbound and outbound calls?

Yes. We support both inbound and outbound calling via API. You can purchase local or international numbers, receive inbound calls, initiate outbound calls, and apply routing rules.

What’s the difference between SMS and MMS?

SMS is text-only messaging, ideal for OTPs, alerts, and notifications. MMS lets you send media—like images and rich content—great for promotions, product photos, and brand-forward customer communication. Both can support two-way messaging depending on your workflow.

Can I send and receive messages (two-way messaging)?

Yes. Our messaging APIs support one-way and two-way workflows. We enable you to receive replies, process inbound messages via webhooks, and build conversational experiences for customer support, order updates, and engagement campaigns.

What number types do you support (long code, short code, toll-free)?

We support long codes, short codes, and toll-free numbers (availability depends on country/region). We’ll recommend the best option based on your use case—OTP, marketing campaigns, support messaging, or high-volume notifications.

How do you handle throughput, latency, and delivery reliability at scale?

Our platform is designed for high throughput and low latency. We provide delivery and call status tracking through logs and real-time webhooks, so your team can see what happened (queued → sent → delivered/failed) and troubleshoot quickly.
